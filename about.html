    About Us

    At the intersection of AI and human intelligence, we build solutions that empower people, advance knowledge, and transform society.

    Who We Are

    The Center for Cognitive Intelligence and AI Systems (CIAS) at Prince Mohammed Bin Fahd University (PMU) is an international research and innovation hub dedicated to advancing Human-AI Institutional Development in Higher Education, aligned with Saudi Arabia's Vision 2030.

    CIAS brings together researchers from across the world to collaboratively enhance the human and societal experience through ethical, human-centered, and AI-driven innovation. The Center integrates frontier agentic AI systems, cognitive science, computational social science, and interdisciplinary humanities to design transformative solutions for education, healthcare, governance, and society.

    Research at CIAS is structured into five interconnected thematic tracks, each led by domain experts. These tracks collectively ensure that AI innovation remains ethical, inclusive, interdisciplinary, and societally impactful, while strengthening PMU's position as a global leader in Human-AI innovation.

    Mission

    Aligned with Saudi Arabia's Vision 2030 pillars of human-capital development, knowledge-economy growth, and sustainable transformation, CIAS integrates frontier agentic AI systems with advancements in cognitive sciences to generate ethical, human-centered, and institutionally transformative research. The center delivers tangible impact across education, social development, precision health systems, and the natural sciences, fostering innovation that shapes a more equitable future.

    Vision

    CIAS leads research and development in the integration of AI and cognitive sciences through a human-agency first model. We center ethical responsibility, interdisciplinary innovation, and real-world societal impact, ensuring that technological advancement augments—but never replaces—human creativity, judgment, and accountability. Our goal is to foster AI systems that enhance human potential while addressing complex societal challenges with integrity and practical relevance.

    FIVE THEMATIC TRACKS

    Research Tracks

    01

    Cognitive Intelligence & Inclusive Learning

    Lead Researcher: Dr. Arifi Noman Mohammed Waked

    This track explores the biological, cognitive, and computational foundations of human learning and intelligence. It focuses on enhancing educational access through AI-driven adaptive systems that support diverse and marginalized populations.

    Key Focus Areas

    • •Cognitive modeling of learning processes and brain-behavior interaction
    • •AI-driven inclusive education for learners with disabilities (ASD, ADHD, hearing impairment, etc.)
    • •Support systems for English language learners and lifelong learners
    • •Trauma-informed and neurodiversity-aware learning frameworks
    • •AI-assisted cognitive training for developmental and acquired conditions

    Strategic Aims

    • •Establish collaborations with national and international disability and education authorities
    • •Develop AI-based assistive learning systems grounded in evidence-based pedagogy
    • •Enhance inclusive access to cognitive and educational development tools
    • •Strengthen partnerships with medical and educational institutions
    02

    Social Dynamics & Elicitive Learning

    Lead Researcher: Dr. Alia Amir

    This track examines the intersection of AI, ethics, culture, religion, language, and societal transformation in the context of Saudi Vision 2030 and global digital transformation.

    Key Focus Areas

    • •AI ethics, governance, and value-aligned systems
    • •Islamic values, cultural identity, and digital society transformation
    • •Language, communication, and human interaction in AI environments
    • •Philosophy, sociology, and psychology of AI-mediated societies
    • •Human-centered and culturally responsive AI design

    Strategic Aims

    • •Foster interdisciplinary dialogue on AI ethics and societal values
    • •Study AI's role in shaping human communication and behavior
    • •Promote culturally grounded AI governance frameworks
    • •Advance methodological innovation in human–AI social research
    03

    AI Integration in Education & Healthcare

    Lead Researcher: Dr. Jose P. Cerin

    This track pioneers agentic AI systems for educational governance and precision healthcare, combining technical excellence with ethical, human-centered innovation.

    Key Focus Areas

    • •AI-driven educational governance and institutional intelligence
    • •Personalized and adaptive learning ecosystems
    • •Precision healthcare and hyper-personalized (N-of-1) medicine
    • •Multi-agent systems for clinical decision support
    • •Translational AI for education and healthcare systems

    Strategic Aims

    • •Develop scalable Human-AI systems for education and healthcare
    • •Improve institutional efficiency through agentic AI workflows
    • •Enable predictive, personalized, and adaptive interventions
    • •Strengthen international collaborations in applied AI systems
    04

    Mathematical Foundations & Scientific Inquiry

    Lead Researcher: Dr. Najma Saleem

    This track advances the mathematical, statistical, and computational foundations required for AI systems and scientific discovery.

    Key Focus Areas

    • •Mathematical modeling of complex physical and cognitive systems
    • •Computational science and AI-driven numerical methods
    • •Statistical learning, optimization, and data-driven modeling
    • •Interdisciplinary applications in engineering, physics, and biology
    • •AI integration in STEM education and scientific reasoning

    Strategic Aims

    • •Promote interdisciplinary research linking mathematics and AI
    • •Strengthen scientific literacy and analytical thinking in education
    • •Develop computational tools for real-world scientific challenges
    • •Integrate mathematical reasoning into AI-driven education systems
    05

    Transdisciplinary Humanities & Societal Studies

    Lead Researcher: Dr. Edyta Wolny-Ibrahim

    This track explores the role of humanities, culture, and social sciences in shaping ethical AI systems and understanding societal transformation.

    Key Focus Areas

    • •Cultural transformation in the age of AI
    • •Islamic jurisprudence, ethics, and digital societies
    • •Human behavior, identity, and meaning-making in AI environments
    • •Humanities-driven AI governance frameworks
    • •Interdisciplinary social science approaches to AI systems

    Strategic Aims

    • •Strengthen cultural and ethical grounding of AI systems
    • •Promote interdisciplinary humanities–AI research
    • •Analyze societal transformation in digital ecosystems
    • •Ensure AI systems align with human values and cultural identity
