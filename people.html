    JC

    Dr. Jose P. Cerin

    Lead Researcher

    Lead Researcher: AI Integration in Education and Healthcare

    Dr. Jose P. Cerin is a Full Professor at Prince Mohammad Bin Fahd University with a PhD in Philosophy from Spain and postdoctoral fellowships at Cambridge and Oxford Universities. His expertise spans computational social sciences, philosophy of science, and human–AI systems. His research focuses on AI integration in education and healthcare, ethical governance, leadership, and human–AI collaboration. He has authored 19 books and over 100 publications, including more than 50 peer-reviewed articles, and is widely recognized for contributions to methodology, innovation, and interdisciplinary research.

    AA

    Dr. Alia Amir

    Lead Researcher

    Lead Researcher: Social Dynamics and Elicitive Learning

    Dr. Alia Amir is an Associate Professor in Language, Culture, and Communication and Research Coordinator for Social Dynamics and Elicitive Learning at Prince Mohammad Bin Fahd University. Her interdisciplinary research explores linguistics, social interaction, and educational communication within AI-mediated environments. She focuses on how language, culture, and cognition shape human interaction and learning processes. Her work examines how humans and intelligent systems jointly construct meaning, with particular attention to ethics, identity, and the evolving role of AI in socially embedded educational contexts.

    NS

    Dr. Najma Saleem

    Lead Researcher

    Lead Researcher: Mathematical Foundations and Scientific Inquiry in Education

    Dr. Najma Saleem is an Associate Professor of Mathematics and Research Coordinator for Mathematical Foundations and Scientific Inquiry in Education at Prince Mohammad Bin Fahd University. Her expertise lies in applied mathematics, computational modeling, fluid dynamics, nanofluids, and physiological flow systems. She has extensive experience in interdisciplinary research, particularly in thermo-biofluid dynamics, electroosmotic transport, and energy-efficient system design. Her work integrates sustainable engineering principles and advanced mathematical modeling. She has published widely in international journals and has received multiple research productivity and academic excellence awards.

    EW

    Dr. Edyta Wolny-Ibrahim

    Lead Researcher

    Lead Researcher: Transdisciplinary and Humanities Studies

    Dr. Edyta Wolny-Ibrahim is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Sciences and Human Studies at Prince Mohammad Bin Fahd University. She holds a PhD in Humanities (Literary Studies) from the University of Warsaw, with research focusing on Egypt, Islamic education, and cultural identity. Her interests include Arab culture, globalization, Saudi Vision 2030, and contemporary social change in the Arab world. She teaches Modern Standard Arabic and Egyptian dialect and has received recognition for promoting Arabic language and culture internationally.

    SENIOR AFFILIATES

    Senior Affiliates

    Our senior affiliates bring academic excellence, research leadership, and diverse expertise that strengthen CIAS and advance our mission.

    SA

    Dr. Siddiqua Aamir

    Assistant Professor · Department of Humanities and Social Sciences

    Expertise: Psychology and Student Wellbeing

    Dr. Siddiqua Aamir is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Humanities and Social Sciences at Prince Mohammad Bin Fahd University, Saudi Arabia. She earned her Ph.D. in Psychology from the University of Peshawar, Pakistan. She has extensive experience in teaching, research, counseling, and clinical psychology across academic and healthcare settings. Her research focuses on mental health, student wellbeing, self-efficacy, and learning experiences, and she has published in numerous journals and presented at global conferences. Her current work emphasizes sustainable learning, soft skills development, and psychological factors shaping higher education outcomes.

    DA

    Dr. Dilsad Ahmed

    Assistant Professor · Department of Humanities and Social Sciences

    Expertise: Physical Activity, Sport Psychology and SEM

    Dr. Dilsad Ahmed holds four degrees from leading universities in India and an M.S. in Kinesiology from Oregon State University, USA, where he served as a Graduate Teaching Assistant. He completed postdoctoral training at the University of Macau and the University of Alberta as a Mitacs Elevate Fellow, and was a Visiting Scholar at the Korea Institute of Sport Science. Currently he is an Assistant Professor at PMU, Saudi Arabia. With over 60 indexed publications, his research focuses on physical activity, sport psychology, and structural equation modelling, contributing to education, public health, and interdisciplinary research.

    KI

    Dr. Kelechukwu Ihemere

    Associate Professor · Department of Humanities and Social Sciences

    Expertise: Sociolinguistics, Multilingualism and World Englishes

    Dr. Kelechukwu Ihemere is an Associate Professor of Linguistics and Senior Fellow of Advance HE (UK). He holds an MA and PhD in Linguistics from Newcastle University, UK. His research focuses on sociolinguistics, multilingualism, language contact, and World Englishes. He studies how social structures, mobility, and ideology shape language variation, identity, and power in multilingual contexts. Using ethnography, corpus analysis, and sociophonetics, he examines emergent language practices and contact-induced change. His work highlights speaker agency in global communication and has implications for education, language policy, and digital communication systems.

    Mr. Alaa Alhasawi

    Junior Research Affiliate

    Mechanical Engineering

    Expertise: AI in Education and Human Behavior

    Alaa Alhawsawi is a mechanical engineering student with a strong interest in interdisciplinary research, particularly in technology, education, ethics, and human behavior.     HA

    Mr. Hamed Alghamdi

    Junior Research Affiliate

    Mechanical Engineering

    Expertise: Cognitive Psychology and AI

    Hamed Alghamdi is a mechanical engineering student with a strong interest in psychology, particularly cognitive dissonance, and its intersection with artificial intelligence. His research experience includes studying cognitive dissonance, examining how conflicting beliefs and attitudes shape human reasoning and decision-making, with an interest in how AI systems can be used to model and predict these cognitive patterns. He is especially curious about how large language models interact with human psychology, including how AI-driven tools influence belief formation, reasoning, and self-perception. He enjoys approaching questions from multiple disciplinary angles, combining technical thinking from engineering with psychological insight and an interest in AI’s growing role in understanding human behavior. As a Junior Research Affiliate, he hopes to further develop his research skills, contribute to interdisciplinary projects, and explore how AI can be leveraged to study and address cognitive biases and decision-making processes.
