ABOUT US

Who We Are

The Center for Cognitive Intelligence and AI Systems (CIAS) at Prince Mohammed Bin Fahd University (PMU) is an international research and innovation hub dedicated to advancing Human-AI Institutional Development in Higher Education, aligned with Saudi Arabia's Vision 2030.

CIAS brings together researchers from across the world to collaboratively enhance the human and societal experience through ethical, human-centered, and AI-driven innovation, integrating frontier agentic AI systems, cognitive science, computational social science, and interdisciplinary humanities.

WHAT WE DO

Five Interconnected Research Tracks

Each track is led by domain experts, ensuring AI innovation remains ethical, inclusive, interdisciplinary, and societally impactful.

Cognitive Intelligence & Inclusive Learning

Enhancing educational access through AI-driven adaptive systems for diverse and marginalized populations.

Social Dynamics & Elicitive Learning

Examining AI, ethics, culture, religion, and language in the context of Saudi Vision 2030.

AI Integration in Education & Healthcare

Pioneering agentic AI systems for educational governance and precision healthcare.

Mathematical Foundations & Scientific Inquiry

Advancing the mathematical, statistical, and computational foundations for AI and scientific discovery.

Transdisciplinary Humanities & Societal Studies

Exploring humanities, culture, and social sciences in shaping ethical AI systems.
