    Strategic Collaboration in Artificial Intelligence, Education, and Digital Innovation with Seoul National University (SNU), Republic of Korea

    Read the full announcement

    The Center for Cognitive Intelligence and AI Systems (CIAS) at Prince Mohammad Bin Fahd University (PMU) has established a strategic collaboration with the Center for Humanities and Information and AI Media Contents Office at Seoul National University (SNU), Republic of Korea. This partnership aims to advance academic excellence, human development, and lifelong learning through interdisciplinary collaboration in artificial intelligence, education, and digital innovation.

    The collaboration focuses on key areas including AI-driven pedagogical innovation, personalized learning systems, contextualized learning approaches, transformative education, leadership development, and global knowledge exchange. Through joint research, academic initiatives, and professional development activities, both institutions aim to explore innovative approaches that integrate artificial intelligence with human-centered education and problem-solving.

    This partnership also supports research and knowledge exchange on the intersection of technology, culture, and education, including studies related to digital humanities, AI applications, and the broader impact of emerging technologies on society. Together, PMU and SNU are committed to fostering innovation, empowering future-ready learners, and building a bridge between academic and technological advancements across Saudi Arabia and Korea.
